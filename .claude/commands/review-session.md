description: Coach review of my latest Battlegrounds session (from bg-coach's verified facts)
---

Run `uv run bgcoach facts $ARGUMENTS` in the bg-coach folder (no arguments = my last session;
`--last 10` = last 10 games) and write my coaching review from its output.

Rules:
- Use ONLY numbers that appear in the facts output. Never invent card effects, meta claims or stats.
  If you mention a card or hero, it must be in the facts.
- Say how many games each point rests on; one game is an anecdote, say so.
- "meta" numbers are correlations at my MMR, not rules.

Structure (short, in plain words, no jargon):
1. **Session in one line**: games, average place, MMR change.
2. **The 3 decisions to learn from** (from "Decisions to learn from"): for each, game + round, what I
   did, what was better, and the calculation shown. Concrete ("round 7: you bought X (4.36) while Y
   (4.11, fits your Pirates) was affordable"), never generic.
3. **Context from "Your stats compared"**, only as context (e.g. leveling late may have been right
   at low HP); do not turn these into rules.
4. **One concrete thing to do next session**, phrased as a habit I can check while playing
   (e.g. "at the start of round 6, if the overlay shows board under 80%, spend the turn on stats").
5. Optional: one thing I'm doing well and should keep.

Keep it under 250 words. Offer to open a specific game in the app (`http://127.0.0.1:8765/#game=<file>`).
